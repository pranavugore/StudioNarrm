Studio Narrm is a Melbourne studio that designs and builds data dashboards, workflow automation, AI agents and custom apps. This is where we write about that work in plain English.

## What we'll write about

- **Data and dashboards:** turning the numbers you already collect into something you can read at a glance.
- **Automation:** taking the repetitive, copy-and-paste parts of a job off people's plates.
- **AI agents:** where they help a small team today, and where they don't yet.
- **Getting ready for AI search:** making sure assistants like ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity can find and describe your business accurately.

## Who it's for

Small businesses, clinics, non-profits and teams who want practical answers rather than hype. Each article aims to leave you with one thing you can try this week.
